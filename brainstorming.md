## Favorite Existing Apps - Categorized and Evaluated
### TikTok
- **Category:** Social Media
- **Mobile:** Uses phone camera to post videos and go live
- **Story:** Allows users to express themselve, their hobbies, and life events to there friends, family, and followers through short videos and live streams
- **Market:** This app is available to everybody, the feed is created based on your content consumption, meaning it tries to tailor to your intrests
- **Habit:** Users can post as much as they want and doom scroll infinitly

### Discord
- **Category:** Social and Collaboration
- **Mobile:** Uses phone camera to stream to others and uses the phone microphone to talk to friends via a voice call
- **Story:** Allows users to connect with friends in a channel, group chat, of direct message. Providing a collaborative space where users can interact and play games with their friends
- **Market:** This app is available to everybody, Users can make their own servers for just them and their friends or go out and join servers that are built around their interests and hobbies
- **Habit:** Users can chat as much as they want talk to people for however long they want. This app relies heavily on user to user interation so no doomscroll or addictive component for solo use

### Chipotle App
- **Category:** Food and Service
- **Mobile:** Can find chipotle stores near the user, making it easier to place orders
- **Story:** Creates a platform for customers to order and pay online so their food is ready in advanced, while accumulating points for rewards
- **Market:** This app targets Chipotle Customers, especially those who frequent the stores
- **Habit:** No addicitive or doomscrolling features, this app is a service designed for making, ording food easier and more convenient

### Instagram
- **Category:** Social Media
- **Mobile:** Uses phone camera to post photos, videos, and go live
- **Story:** Allows users to express themselve, their hobbies, and life events to there friends and family through photos and videos
- **Market:** This app is available to everybody, people follow who they are interested and see content based on who they follow and what their interests are
- **Habit:** Users can post as much as they want and doom scroll their feed, the explore tab, or shorts for endless content

### Spotify
- **Category:** Music
- **Mobile:** Uses phone to play music, typically using blue tooth while on the go
- **Story:** Allows users to publish and listen to music
- **Market:** This app is available to everybody, people can make their own playlists or find playlists that were shared by others to listen to the music they like
- **Habit:** Users can create as many playlists as they want and this app is typically used everyday, to play and listen to music while in the car or on the go

### My Singing Monsters
- **Category:** Video Game
- **Mobile:** N/A
- **Story:** Users place and breed monsters that create a song. Progressing to different worlds and discovering different monsters to create new songs.

### Pokemon Go
- **Category:** AR Video Game
- **Mobile:** Uses GPS to track users movements, letting players play the game in the real world
- **Story:** Users walk around and explore in hopes of finding rare pokemon. They can also collaborate with other players to takle events and other challenges for rewards
- **Market:** This app is available to everybody, it promotes going outside to explore new pokemon and take on new challenges with friends or by ones self
- **Habit:** Users typically log on while taking walks or during events, promoting healthy user habits

### SnapChat
- **Category:** Social Media
- **Mobile:** Uses phone camera to post photos and videos. Can also allow it to access you location and share your location with friends
- **Story:** Allows users to express themselve, their hobbies, and life events to there friends and family through photos, videos, or groupchats

### Netflix
